Decision 2: Color

Now that we are leaning into a Data-Dense & Minimalist feel, we need to choose the color palette. This decides our brand/primary color (used for buttons, links, and active states), our grayscale ramp (used for text, backgrounds, and borders), and our semantic colors (success/warning/error).

Option 1: Slate & Blue (Recommended)

A standard, professional blue primary with cool "slate" grays. Highly legible, familiar, and works well for data visualization. Keeps semantic colors standard (green for success).

Decision 3: Typography

With our Forest & Stone color palette chosen, we now need to pick the font families. Because you chose a data-dense layout, a font that is legible at small sizes and has clear tabular numbers (so numbers align vertically in tables) is critical.

Option 1: Inter (Recommended)

A modern font specifically designed for computer screens. Extremely legible at small sizes, making it perfect for dense dashboards and data tables.

Decision 5: Shape & Elevation

Now we define how UI elements are shaped—specifically, how rounded their corners are, and whether we use solid borders or drop shadows to separate them from the background.

Option 1: Sharp & Flat
Square corners (0px radius), strong borders, and zero shadows. Very industrial and purely functional.

Option 2: Rounded & Subtle (Recommended)
Slightly rounded corners (4px radius), subtle borders, and very faint shadows. This perfectly matches the "modern admin dashboard" aesthetic.

Option 3: Soft & Elevated
Highly rounded corners (12px radius), no borders, and pronounced drop shadows. Feels much more like a consumer app than an enterprise tool.
